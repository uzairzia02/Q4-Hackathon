# Hardware Requirements

This course is technically demanding, sitting at the intersection of Physics Simulation, Visual Perception, and Generative AI. The hardware you have will determine your learning path.

## 1. The "Digital Twin" Workstation (Ideal)

This is the primary machine for simulation and training.

- **GPU**: NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti (12GB VRAM) or higher. An RTX 3090 or 4090 (24GB VRAM) is recommended for the best experience.
- **CPU**: Intel Core i7 (13th Gen+) or AMD Ryzen 9.
- **RAM**: 64 GB DDR5 (32 GB is the absolute minimum).
- **OS**: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS (native or dual-boot is mandatory).

## 2. Cloud-Based Alternative

If you do not have access to an RTX workstation, you can use a cloud-based environment.
- **Instance Type**: AWS g5.2xlarge or similar.
- **Note**: Detailed setup guides for cloud environments will be provided.

## 3. The "Physical AI" Edge Kit

This kit is for deploying your AI models to a physical device.

- **The Brain**: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (8GB) or Orin NX (16GB).
- **The Eyes**: Intel RealSense D435i or D455.
- **The Ears**: ReSpeaker USB Mic Array v2.0.
